# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The **omicgene** community is dedicated to providing an open, welcoming, and respectful environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or affiliation. We foster an inclusive space for computational biologists, machine learning researchers, bioinformaticians, and software engineers to collaborate on genomic modeling and antimicrobial resistance research.

## Community Standards

We expect all contributors, maintainers, and community participants to uphold high standards of professional and scientific conduct:

- **Respect and Collegiality:** Treat every participant with courtesy and respect. Constructive disagreement and scientific debate are encouraged, but personal attacks, demeaning comments, and harassment are not tolerated.
- **Scientific Integrity:** Strive for transparency, reproducibility, and rigorous documentation in all contributions, whether related to datasets, algorithms, or literature.
- **Inclusivity:** Welcome newcomers and support individuals of all skill levels. Help others learn and succeed.
- **Constructive Collaboration:** Focus feedback on code, algorithms, data methodology, and ideas rather than individuals. Offer actionable suggestions and accept feedback graciously.
- **Responsible Handling of Data:** Respect intellectual property, licensing, and any privacy or ethical constraints associated with biological or genomic data.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behaviors include, but are not limited to:
- Harassing, derogatory, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or insulting/disparaging remarks.
- Publishing private personal information (doxxing) without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or community channels.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate or harmful in a professional open-source context.

## Reporting and Enforcement

If you witness or experience behavior that violates these guidelines, please report it to the project maintainers:

- **Contact:** `darrehanrasool@gmail.com`
- You may also reach out directly to repository maintainers via GitHub.

All reports will be handled promptly, discreetly, and confidentially. Maintainers will review the incident, investigate fairly, and take appropriate action, which may include:
- Issuing a private or public warning.
- Editing or removing offending comments, issues, or contributions.
- Temporary or permanent suspension of participation in the project and associated repositories.

## Scope

These guidelines apply across all spaces managed by the omicgene project, including code repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussion boards, and any official communication channels.
