## 🔬 Detailed Annotations

### 1. MSDeepAMR: antimicrobial resistance prediction based on deep neural networks and transfer learning (2024)
- **Authors:** Frontiers in Microbiology
- **Venue / DOI:** *Frontiers in Microbiology*, [10.3389/fmicb.2024.1361795](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmicb.2024.1361795)
- **Online Article:** [Read on Frontiers](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/microbiology/articles/10.3389/fmicb.2024.1361795/full)
- **Summary:** Introduces MSDeepAMR, a deep learning architecture incorporating transfer learning to predict antimicrobial resistance across multiple bacterial species.
- **File:** [2024_MSDeepAMR_TransferLearning_Frontiers.pdf](2024_MSDeepAMR_TransferLearning_Frontiers.pdf)

### 2. The role of artificial intelligence and machine learning in predicting and combating antimicrobial resistance (2025)
- **Authors:** Hazrat Bilal, Muhammad Nadeem Khan, Sabir Khan, Muhammad Shafiq, Wenjie Fang, Rahat Ullah Khan, et al.
- **Venue / DOI:** *Computational and Structural Biotechnology Journal*, Vol. 27, pp. 423–439, [10.1016/j.csbj.2025.01.006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.csbj.2025.01.006)
- **Online Article:** [Read on Science Partner Journals](https://spj.science.org/doi/full/10.1016/j.csbj.2025.01.006) | [PubMed Central PMC11791014](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11791014/)
- **Summary:** Comprehensive review evaluating supervised, unsupervised, and deep learning paradigms across clinical datasets, genomic sequences, and EHRs for AMR forecasting and antimicrobial stewardship.
- **File:** [2025_AI_ML_PredictingCombatingAMR_CSBJ.pdf](2025_AI_ML_PredictingCombatingAMR_CSBJ.pdf)

### 3. DeepARG: a deep learning approach for predicting antibiotic resistance genes from metagenomic data (2018)
- **Authors:** Gustavo Arango-Argoty, Eric Garner, Amy Pruden, Lenwood S. Heath, Peter Vikesland, Liqing Zhang
- **Venue / DOI:** *Microbiome*, [10.1186/s40168-018-0401-z](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40168-018-0401-z)
- **Online Article:** [Read on Springer Nature](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40168-018-0401-z)
- **Summary:** Deep learning framework (DeepARG-SS and DeepARG-LS) predicting antibiotic resistance genes from metagenomic short reads and full-length sequences with high recall and precision.
- **File:** [2018_DeepARG_MetagenomicAMR_Microbiome.pdf](2018_DeepARG_MetagenomicAMR_Microbiome.pdf)

### 4. Prediction of antimicrobial resistance based on whole-genome sequencing and machine learning (2022)
- **Authors:** Yunxiao Ren, Trinad Chakraborty, Swapnil Doijad, Linda Falgenhauer, et al.
- **Venue / DOI:** *Bioinformatics*, Vol. 38, Issue 2, pp. 325–334, [10.1093/bioinformatics/btab681](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btab681)
- **Online Article:** [Read on Oxford Academic](https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/article/38/2/325/6382301)
- **Summary:** Systematic evaluation of Random Forests, CNNs, SVMs, and Logistic Regression on whole-genome sequencing data with various genomic encodings (FCGR, one-hot, label).
- **File:** [2022_Ren_WGS_MachineLearningAMR_Bioinformatics.pdf](2022_Ren_WGS_MachineLearningAMR_Bioinformatics.pdf)

### 5. Artificial intelligence in predicting pathogenic microorganisms’ antimicrobial resistance: challenges, progress, and prospects (2024)
- **Authors:** Frontiers in Cellular and Infection Microbiology
- **Venue / DOI:** *Frontiers in Cellular and Infection Microbiology*, [10.3389/fcimb.2024.1482186](https://doi.org/10.3389/fcimb.2024.1482186)
- **Online Article:** [Read on Frontiers](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/cellular-and-infection-microbiology/articles/10.3389/fcimb.2024.1482186/full)
- **Summary:** In-depth examination of contemporary breakthroughs, data biases, and clinical translation challenges in deploying AI for pathogen AMR prediction.
- **File:** [2024_AI_PredictingPathogenAMR_Frontiers.pdf](2024_AI_PredictingPathogenAMR_Frontiers.pdf)
